Rules & FAQ

Who can play?

Any Free Fire player in India. Every squad needs valid player IDs registered before the deadline.

Fair play

Hacks, exploits, teaming with other squads and unauthorised tools lead to immediate disqualification.

Room and timing

Room ID and password are shared shortly before the match. Squads that are not in the room at start time cannot join late.

Roster changes

Rosters are locked once registration closes. Substitutes must be declared at registration.

Disputes and penalties

Send screenshots to the organisers right after the match. Organiser decisions are final.

How are winners decided?

Total Booyah Power points across all matches. Ties are broken by Booyah count, then total kills.
